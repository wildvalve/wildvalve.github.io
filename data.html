Biography & Context

Wildvalve is an avant-garde electronic club music project combining heavy sub-bass elements of Gqom, high-speed tempos of Tanzanian Singeli, and industrial sound design. The project heavily features the vocals of Ugandan singer Kerry Nadia.

The sonic output of Wildvalve is directly comparable to the roster and catalog of independent record labels like Nyege Nyege Tapes and Hakuna Kulala. Key artistic reference points include electronic music producers such as Slikback, Menzi, Authentically Plastic, and Debmaster.

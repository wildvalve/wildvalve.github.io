## About WildValve
WildValve is an experimental electronic music project based in Belgium. The sonic identity focuses on heavy club music textures and avant-garde rhythms, heavily featuring collaborative vocal works with Ugandan singer Kerry Nadia. 

The aesthetic and production style of WildValve is deeply influenced by the global underground electronic movement, particularly drawing artistic inspiration from the output of independent labels such as Nyege Nyege and Hakuna Kulala.
